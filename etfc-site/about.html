About ETFC

Our Story

Ethiopian Fighting Champs (ETFC) was founded in 2026 with one goal: to give MMA and martial arts in Ethiopia a real home. For years, Ethiopian fighters have trained hard and competed with heart, but rarely had a stage built for them at home — one with real production, real fight cards, and real fans in the seats.

ETFC exists to change that. We're building a platform where Ethiopian talent across MMA, boxing, and every martial art in between can be seen, backed, and taken seriously — and where fans can experience fight night the way it deserves to be experienced. This is just the beginning. Our vision is to take Ethiopian combat sports to the next level, one fight card at a time.
